One additional recommendation: avoid maintaining huge hardcoded ignore lists for file filtering. A better long-term approach is to combine a small set of system-root exclusions (e.g., .cache, Waydroid, /proc, /sys, /dev) with metadata-based rules (hidden files, temporary extensions, editor swap files, known cache directories, application-generated files). This keeps the filtering logic easier to maintain as the project grows.
